From the problem to a proposal

Why islanded AI plants need a supervisory layer above the stabiliser, and what an eight-week Phase 1 study with Bergen Engines On Land would deliver.

Three layers of control

The layer above the stabiliser

A fast stabiliser answers the milliseconds and the engine governors answer the seconds. Someone still has to decide how many engines run, how much reserve to hold for the next step, and what the data centre is about to do.

Layer 0Fast stabiliserSub-millisecond to 1 sAbsorbs and injects power during AI load steps
Layer 1Engine control0.1 s to 10 sGovernors hold frequency and share load
Layer 2Supervisory control1 s to hoursEngine commitment, reserve policy, stabiliser energy state, signals from the data-centre scheduler

A dynamic plant model calibrated on real load-step tests, run against measured AI training load profiles for two reference plants.
